The short version

  • MUG is for people 14 and older. 14–17 and 18–23 are kept in separate age groups that never mix.
  • We never sell or rent your data, MUG has no ads, and we never build profiles of you for marketing.
  • Mugs (your photos) are deleted after 7 days unless you save them to your MUGBOOK.
  • People who pick you never see your name, and you never see theirs, unless you're both 18+ and pick each other.
  • Your photo only goes to our AI partner if you say yes, and it is never used to train AI.
  • You can see, download, fix or delete your data at any time. Delete your account in Settings and it's gone within 30 days.

Who we are and what this covers

Where a law in your place gives you more protection, we follow it.

How picks and clues work

MUG lets you pick, and be picked, without names attached. Here is exactly what other people can see:

  • When you pick someone, they see the vibe you chose and a free hint: whether you're a girl or a guy, and whether you're from their school or campus. Never your name or handle.
  • Clues: if they have Guilty, they can also see your year, the first letter of your name and whether you're in their crew. Never your name or handle.
  • Mutual picks (18+ only): if two people 18 or older pick each other, MUG reveals them to each other and a chat opens.
  • Passing someone in the lineup is never shown to anyone.
  • Your guesses about how people see you are private to you.
  • Links of users under 18 can only be answered by signed-in MUG users aged 14 to 17 from the same school.

Picks are hidden from other users, but not from us. We can see who picked whom when we need to investigate a report, keep MUG safe or respond to valid legal process.

Your photos, AI and automated decisions

  • You choose. Before your first AI read, MUG asks your permission to send your mug to our AI provider, [AI PROVIDER]. You can say no and use everything else in MUG, and you can change your mind in Settings at any time.

Teens, parents and guardians

Many people on MUG are teenagers. We design for that first, following the Children's Privacy Code and the US state laws that protect minors:

  • 14 and up only. MUG isn't for anyone under 14. If we learn that someone under 14 has an account, we delete it and their information.
  • Protected by default. For users under 18: profiles are private and can't be found by search engines, only people 14–17 from their own school can see them or answer their link, location stays off, quiet hours are on, and there's no chat with strangers and no crush or dating features.
  • No ads, no profiling, no selling. We never show ads, sell or share teens' information for advertising, or profile teens for marketing.
  • No tricks. We don't use design tricks to push teens to share more or to make privacy choices harder.
  • Parental consent where the law requires it. In some places, users under 16 or under 18 need a parent's or guardian's consent before they can have an account. Where that applies, we ask for it at sign-up, and a parent can withdraw it at any time.
  • Purchases go through Apple's and Google's parental approval settings, such as Ask to Buy.
  • Parents and guardians can email privacy@nospoon.so to ask what we hold about their teen, have it corrected or deleted, or close the account. We'll confirm you're the parent or guardian before acting.

Read more for parents at mug.wtf/parents.

Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See your information and get a copy, including in a structured, commonly used format you can take elsewhere.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your account and information: Settings → Delete account, or email us.
  • Withdraw consent for AI reads, notifications or marketing in Settings or your device settings, at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything done before.
  • Ask for a person to review a decision made by automated moderation.
  • Complain to us and to a privacy regulator.

To use these rights, email privacy@nospoon.so from the email or phone number on your account, or use Settings. We'll confirm who you are before sharing or deleting anything, and reply within 30 days. It's free. You can ask someone, such as a parent, to make a request for you; we'll confirm they have your permission. We'll never treat you differently, or charge you more, for using your rights.

If you live in Canada

  • Under PIPEDA, you can access and correct your information and withdraw consent. If we don't resolve your concern, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
  • Quebec: you also have the right to have information about you de-indexed or not disseminated where the law allows, to be told when a decision about you was made only by automated processing and to have it reviewed, and to data portability. You can complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec. Before we transfer information outside Quebec, we assess whether it will be adequately protected.
  • British Columbia and Alberta: where your provincial privacy law applies, you have the rights it gives you, and you can complain to your provincial privacy commissioner.

If you live in the United States

  • State privacy rights. Depending on your state (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas, Virginia and others), you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete and get a copy of your information, and to opt out of its sale, targeted advertising and profiling. MUG does none of those, but you can still make any request.
  • Appeals. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing us with "Appeal" in the subject. We'll answer within 45 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.
  • California. In the last 12 months we collected the categories listed in What we collect (identifiers, customer records, commercial information, internet activity, approximate geolocation, audio-visual information and inferences limited to your MUG file), for the purposes in How we use it. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the information of anyone under 16.   • Under 13. MUG isn't directed to children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or under 14). If you believe we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Where your information is stored

NoSpoon is based in Canada, and our providers store and process information in Canada, the United States and the other places listed above. Information stored outside your province or country is protected by our contracts with those providers but may be accessible to courts and authorities there under local law.

How we protect it

We encrypt information in transit and at rest, limit access to the few people who need it, require strong authentication for our systems, keep logs of who accesses data, and delete what we no longer need. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we'll tell you and the regulators as quickly as possible, as the law requires, and we keep a record of every security incident.

Cookies and our website

mug.wtf doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. MUG link pages use a small piece of local storage to remember that you've already answered and to stop bots. Our website host may log your IP address and browser type to keep the site running and secure.
