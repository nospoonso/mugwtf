The short version

  • MUG is for people 14 and older. 14–17 and 18–23 are kept in separate age groups.
  • We never sell your data, and MUG has no ads.
  • Mugs (your photos) are deleted after 7 days unless you save them to your MUGBOOK.
  • People who pick you never see your name, and you never see theirs, unless you both pick each other.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

Who we are

MUG is made by NoSpoon Inc., a British Columbia company that operates from Winnipeg, Manitoba. This policy explains what MUG collects, why, who sees it and what you can do about it. It covers the MUG apps for iPhone and Android and this website.

How picks and clues work

MUG is built so you can be picked, and pick others, without names attached. Here is exactly what other people can see:

  • When you pick someone, they see the vibe you chose and a free hint (whether you're a girl or a guy, and whether you're from their school or campus). They don't see your name.
  • Clues: if they have Guilty, they can also see your year, the first letter of your name and whether you're in their crew. Never your name or handle.
  • Mutual picks: if you both pick each other, MUG reveals you to each other, and a chat opens.
  • Passing in the lineup is never shown to anyone.

Your guess about how people see you is private to you.

Your photos and AI

  • You choose. Before your first AI read, MUG asks your permission to send your mug to our AI provider, [AI PROVIDER]. You can say no and still use MUG.
  • Vibes, not scores. The AI read describes the vibe and choices in a photo (like your fit). It never rates how attractive you are, and it never guesses your age, race, body type, health or mood.
  • No training, no keeping. [AI PROVIDER] processes photos under terms that bar it from keeping them or using them to train its models.
  • Safety checks. Every photo and message passes through automated moderation by [MODERATION PROVIDER] to block nudity, violence and bullying before anyone sees it.
  • Never for ads or profiling. Your photos are never used for advertising, marketing or profiling, by us or anyone else.

Teens and parents

  • MUG is not for anyone under 14. If we learn we have data from someone under 14, we delete it.
  • Users under 18 only see and are seen by others aged 14 to 17, from their own school.
  • For users under 18: no ads, no profiling for marketing, no crush or dating features, and privacy-protective settings by default (only people from their school can answer, quiet hours on).
  • Purchases by users under 18 go through Apple's and Google's parental approval settings.
  • Parents or guardians can email privacy@nospoon.so to ask what we hold about their teen, or to have it deleted.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. We delete your account, mugs, picks and chats within 30 days. You can also ask by email from the address or number on your account.
  • See or correct your data: email privacy@nospoon.so. We reply within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off AI reads or notifications in Settings at any time.
  • Extra rights: depending on where you live (for example Quebec, California or the EU), you may have more rights. Email us and we'll honour them.

Where data is stored and how we protect it

Our providers may store and process data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. We encrypt data in transit, limit who can access it, and delete what we don't need. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll tell you and the regulators as the law requires.
