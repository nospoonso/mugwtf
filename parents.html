What MUG is

MUG is a social app about how people see each other. Your teen takes a daily photo (a "mug"), guesses how others see them, and shares a link. Friends and classmates answer by picking from set, positive vibes like "best aura" or "texts back in 2 min". Your teen then sees how their guess compares to what people actually picked.

Picks are shown without names, so people feel free to be honest. That's also why MUG is built with no way to type a message to a stranger: every vibe is written by us, and none of them are cruel.

How MUG protects teens

  • Age 14 and up. Under-14s can't use MUG, and accounts we find are deleted.
  • Separate age groups. Users 14–17 never see or interact with adults on MUG.
  • Own school only. Teens only see people their age from their own school.
  • No chat for anyone under 18, and no dating or crush features for anyone. MUG is for making friends.
  • No ads, and we never sell data.
  • Photos are checked automatically before anyone sees them, and deleted after 7 days unless your teen saves them.
  • Quiet hours are on by default from 10pm to 7am.

Read more in our child safety standards and safety tools.

Purchases

MUG is free. There's an optional subscription called Guilty that gives clues about who picked you (like their year or the first letter of their name, never their name), plus one-off items like cosmetics. All payments go through Apple or Google, so you can require approval for every purchase with Ask to Buy on iPhone or purchase approvals on Android.

Talking with your teen

  • Ask what vibes they got, and which surprised them. Most teens love sharing this.
  • Remind them that picks are opinions, not facts about who they are.
  • Agree that they'll come to you if anything on MUG, or anywhere online, makes them uncomfortable.
  • Show them how to block and report. Our safety tools page lists every control.
  • Talk about never sending photos to anyone who asks, and that threats to share images are a crime they won't get in trouble for reporting.

If you have a concern

  • Something on MUG about your teen: email safety@nospoon.so. You don't need a MUG account.
  • See or delete your teen's data: email privacy@nospoon.so.
  • Your teen is being threatened or blackmailed over images: contact your local police, and in Canada NeedHelpNow.ca, or anywhere Take It Down, which helps remove images of under-18s from the internet.
  • Immediate danger: call your local emergency number.
