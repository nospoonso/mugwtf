Need help right now?

If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number: 911 in Canada and the US, 112 in the EU and many other countries, 999 in the UK.

Anywhere in the world

  • Find A Helpline: free, confidential crisis lines in your country, by phone, text or chat.
  • Child Helpline International: helplines for young people in more than 130 countries. In many European countries, call 116 111.
  • INHOPE: report online child sexual abuse material to the hotline in your country.

Some local lines

  • Canada and US: call or text 9-8-8. Under 18 in Canada: Kids Help Phone, 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868.
  • UK and Ireland: Samaritans, 116 123. Under 19 in the UK: Childline, 0800 1111.
  • Australia: Lifeline, 13 11 14. Ages 5–25: Kids Helpline, 1800 55 1800.
  • Report online exploitation: Cybertip.ca (Canada), NCMEC CyberTipline (US).

Anywhere in the world

  • Find A Helpline: free, confidential crisis lines in your country, by phone, text or chat
  • Child Helpline International: helplines for young people in more than 130 countries
  • Take It Down: helps remove nude or sexual images taken before you were 18
  • StopNCII: helps stop intimate images of adults being shared
  • INHOPE: report child sexual abuse material to the hotline in your country

Canada

  • 9-8-8: suicide crisis helpline, call or text 9-8-8
  • Kids Help Phone: support for young people, 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868
  • Cybertip.ca: report online sexual exploitation of children
  • NeedHelpNow.ca: help when a sexual image of you has been shared

United States

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: report online exploitation of children
  • The Trevor Project: support for LGBTQ+ young people
  • StopBullying.gov: help with bullying and cyberbullying

United Kingdom

  • Childline: for anyone under 19, 0800 1111
  • Samaritans: call 116 123, any time
  • Shout: text SHOUT to 85258
  • CEOP: report online sexual abuse or grooming to the police

Ireland

  • Childline (ISPCC): for anyone under 18
  • Samaritans: call 116 123, any time
  • Hotline.ie: report illegal content online

Australia

  • Kids Helpline: ages 5 to 25, 1800 55 1800
  • Lifeline: call 13 11 14
  • eSafety Commissioner: report cyberbullying and image-based abuse
  • headspace: mental health support for ages 12 to 25

New Zealand

  • 1737: free call or text 1737 to talk to a counsellor
  • Youthline: support for young people
  • Netsafe: help with online harm

Somewhere else?

Use Find A Helpline to find free support where you live, and call your local emergency number if anyone is in danger. You can always email us at safety@nospoon.so too.
