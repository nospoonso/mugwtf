MUG works because people are kind about how they see each other. These are the rules that keep it that way.

The short version

  • Pick people for what you actually see in them. Keep it kind or keep it funny.
  • Only post mugs of yourself.
  • Nothing sexual. Ever. And nothing that sexualizes anyone under 18.
  • Don't try to unmask who picked you outside MUG's own features.
  • Break the rules and you lose features or your account.

The vibe

MUG exists to show people how others see them, and most people are surprised by how good it feels. That only works if everyone plays fair. Pick honestly, keep it kind, and treat the people in your lineup like the real people they are, because they are.

No bullying or shaming

  • Don't use picks, mugs, chats or your MUG card to mock, shame, threaten or gang up on anyone.
  • Don't screenshot someone's mug or picks to make fun of them anywhere else.
  • No hate. Never target someone for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, body or how they look.
  • Don't spread rumours or lies about anyone.

Nothing sexual

  • No nudity, sexual content or sexual comments, in mugs, chats or anywhere else on MUG.
  • Nothing that sexualizes anyone under 18. This gets your account banned and reported. See our child safety standards.
  • Never pressure anyone for photos, and never threaten to share someone's images.

Only you, only real

  • Every mug must be a photo of you, taken by you. No one else's face, no celebrities, no AI-generated faces.
  • Use your real age and your real school or campus. Lying about your age gets your account removed.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else.

Respect people's privacy

  • Don't share anyone's address, phone number, school schedule or other personal information.
  • Don't try to find out who picked you by any way other than the features in MUG: no fake accounts, no pressuring friends, no outside tools.
  • Don't post mugs taken in private places like bathrooms or locker rooms.

Nothing dangerous

  • No threats or violence, and nothing that encourages it.
  • Nothing that promotes suicide, self-harm or eating disorders. If you're struggling, help is here, and you can talk to someone right now.
  • Nothing illegal: no drugs, weapons, alcohol sales or scams.

No spam or selling

  • No ads, promo codes, follow-for-follow, or selling anything.
  • No bots, fake accounts or automated picks.

What happens if you break the rules

We look at what happened and how serious it is. Depending on that, we may:

  • remove the content;
  • warn you;
  • pause features, like picking, posting or your link;
  • ban your account, permanently for serious or repeated violations;
  • report it to the authorities when someone's safety is at risk or the law requires it.

Think we got it wrong? Email support@nospoon.so from the email or phone number on your account and a person will take another look.
