Extra protection for 14–17

These are on for every user under 18, and most can't be turned off:

  • Only people 14–17 from your own school can see you, pick you or answer your link.
  • Mutual picks reveal you only to someone your age at your school who picked you too. You can add each other to your crew. There's no chat.
  • No ads, and no profiling for marketing.
  • Quiet hours on by default.
  • Purchases go through Apple's and Google's parental approval settings, like Ask to Buy.

For 18–23

  • Chats only open on a mutual pick, and you can leave or block at any time.
  • Hidden words lets you filter words and emojis out of your chats. Settings → Chats.
