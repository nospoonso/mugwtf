Need help right now?

If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number: 911 in Canada and the US, 112 in the EU and many other countries, 999 in the UK.

Anywhere in the world

  • Find A Helpline: free, confidential crisis lines in your country, by phone, text or chat.
  • Child Helpline International: helplines for young people in more than 130 countries. In many European countries, call 116 111.
  • INHOPE: report online child sexual abuse material to the hotline in your country.

Some local lines

  • Canada and US: call or text 9-8-8. Under 18 in Canada: Kids Help Phone, 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868.
  • UK and Ireland: Samaritans, 116 123. Under 19 in the UK: Childline, 0800 1111.
  • Australia: Lifeline, 13 11 14. Ages 5–25: Kids Helpline, 1800 55 1800.
  • Report online exploitation: Cybertip.ca (Canada), NCMEC CyberTipline (US).

What MUG promises

your age group only14–17 and 18–23 never see each other. Under-18s only see people from their own school.
real picks onlyNo bots, no fake picks, no fake messages. Every pick comes from a real person.
no face scoresMUG never rates faces or bodies. People pick vibes and choices, like a fit or a playlist.
no free text from strangersPicks are set vibes, not messages. Chats only open when two people pick each other.

How we keep MUG safe

  • Moderation on everything. Every mug and message is checked automatically before anyone sees it, and a human team reviews reports.
  • Kind vibes by design. Every vibe in MUG is written to be positive or playful. There's no way to pick someone for something cruel.
  • Quiet hours. No notifications between 10pm and 7am by default.
  • No crush features for under-18s. Crush picks and "see who likes you" are only for users 18 and older.
  • Honest paywall. Guilty gives clues, never names, and it's clear about price and renewal before you pay.

Your safety tools

  • Hide: remove someone from your lineup.
  • Block: they can't see your mugs, pick you or message you.
  • Report: long-press any mug, pick or message. Reports are anonymous to the person reported.
  • Only friends can answer: turn this on in Settings so only people you know can answer your link.
  • Delete: remove any mug, or your whole account, from Settings.

What happens when you report

  1. The content is checked straight away, and serious reports are prioritized.
  2. If it breaks our rules, we remove it and may warn, limit or ban the account.
  3. Anything involving a child's safety goes to the authorities.
  4. The person you reported is never told who reported them.

For parents

MUG is for ages 14 and up. Teens are kept in their own age group and school, can't use crush features, and see no ads. Purchases go through Apple's and Google's parental approval settings, such as Ask to Buy. If you have a concern about your teen's account, email safety@nospoon.so.

Law enforcement

Law enforcement agencies can send requests for user information to legal@nospoon.so. We respond to valid legal process and act immediately where someone's life or safety is at risk.
