Our commitment

MUG, made by NoSpoon Inc., has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Many people on MUG are teenagers. Protecting them is the first thing we design for, not something we add later.

What is banned

These are banned on MUG for everyone, of every age, with no exceptions:

  • any content that sexually exploits or abuses a minor, including CSAM in any form, real, drawn or AI-generated;
  • sexualizing a minor in any way, including comments, picks or images;
  • grooming: building trust with a minor to exploit or abuse them;
  • sextortion: threatening to share intimate images, or demanding images, money or anything else;
  • asking a minor for sexual content, or to move to another app or meet in person for that purpose;
  • trafficking, or any attempt to buy, sell or arrange contact with a minor.

Breaking these rules means the content is removed, the account is banned permanently, and we report it to the authorities.

How MUG is designed to prevent it

  • Age groups that never mix. 14–17 and 18–23 are separate. Adults can't see, pick or message anyone under 18, and under-18s can't see adults.
  • Own school only for teens. Users under 18 only see and are seen by people their age from their own school.
  • No messages from strangers. Picks are set vibe chips, not free text. Chats only open for users 18+ when two people pick each other. Under-18s have no chat with strangers.
  • No dating or crush features, for anyone. MUG is built for making friends.
  • Photos are checked before anyone sees them. Every mug is scanned automatically for nudity and sexual content and blocked if flagged.
  • Known abuse material is matched. Uploads are checked against databases of known CSAM using [CSAM HASH-MATCHING PROVIDER].
  • Mugs expire. Photos are deleted after 7 days unless the owner saves them.

How to report

  • In the app: long-press the mug, pick, message or profile and tap Report, then choose "child safety". This never leaves the app, and the content is hidden from you straight away.
  • By email: safety@nospoon.so. Parents, guardians, teachers and anyone else can report, even without a MUG account.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first.

Not sure if something crosses the line? Report it anyway. We would much rather review something harmless than miss something serious.

What we do about CSAM

  1. Child safety reports are reviewed first, ahead of every other report.
  2. As soon as we know of CSAM on MUG, by any route (automated detection, a user report, law enforcement or anyone else), we remove it and permanently ban the account.
  3. We report it to the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (Cybertip.ca) and to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline, as the law requires.
  4. We preserve the material securely, as the law requires, so the authorities can investigate, and we cooperate fully with law enforcement.

Laws we follow

We follow the child safety laws that apply to MUG, including Canada's Act respecting the mandatory reporting of Internet child pornography by persons who provide an Internet service and the Criminal Code, and United States law on reporting CSAM to NCMEC (18 U.S.C. § 2258A). We follow local law in the other countries where MUG is available.
